Qualify

Ranked by expected MRR = P(upgrade) × plan price. P(upgrade) comes from the source's learned conversion rate, adjusted by release freshness and the launch-video gap. A lead only counts as qualified if AdaL can make their video, which means it needs a repo or CHANGELOG.
